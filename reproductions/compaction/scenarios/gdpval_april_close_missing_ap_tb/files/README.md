# Aurisic April 2025 month-end financial package

Source files for the April 2025 close. `Aurisic_Financials_3-25-1.xlsx` is last
month's package and the template; every other file is an April schedule, dump
or ledger extract:

- `Aurisic_Financials_3-25-1.xlsx` – March 2025 financials workbook (template: structure, formatting, tab order)
- `Aurisic_Final_TB_4-25-1.txt` – April 2025 general ledger trial balance (system printout)
- `Accr2011-1.xlsx` – Aurisic Global accrual for uninvoiced (#2011)
- `AccrBonus-1.xlsx` – bonus accrual (#2401)
- `AccrMisc-1.xlsx` – miscellaneous accruals (#2410)
- `AP_TB-1.xlsx` – A/P trade subledger by vendor (#2000)
- `AR_Accrual-1.xlsx` – A/R accruals (#1101)
- `Aurisic_Corp_Payrolls_April_2025-1.xlsx` – April corporate payroll runs
- `Aurisic_Prepaid_Expenses_4-25-1.xlsx` – prepaid expenses and prepaid insurance detail
- `Good Insurance Co - Loan.xlsx`, `Good Insurance Co - Loan II.xlsx` – loan schedules (interest accruals)
- `Legal_Dump-1.xlsx` – legal/audit expense detail (#6200)
- `Outstanding_CKs_4-30-25-1.xlsx` – outstanding cheques at 4-30-25 (bank recon)
- `Payroll-1.xlsx` – payroll accrual (#2200)
- `PPD1250-1.xlsx`, `PPD1251-1.xlsx` – prepaid expenses (#1250) and prepaid insurance (#1251) schedules
- `Prof_Fee_Dump-1.xlsx` – professional fees accrual detail (#2404)
- `Rebates-1.xlsx` – vendor rebates (#2005)

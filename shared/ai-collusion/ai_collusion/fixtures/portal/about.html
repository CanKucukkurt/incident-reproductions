About the collection

The Civil Rights Data Collection covers educational access, school climate, and staffing in public schools and districts.

Reporting notes

Collections describe a school year rather than the calendar year. Counselor staffing is expressed as full-time equivalent positions; reported harassment and bullying measures count students reported as victims.

Differences in reporting practices and definitions can affect comparisons. An absent record is not equivalent to a reported zero. Consult the measure reference when interpreting aggregate results.
